# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 2026**

Welcome to VSeekr ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your information when you use the VSeekr mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
When you use VSeekr, we may collect the following types of information:
*   **Account & Profile Information:** Your email address, username, contact name, and optionally your phone number when you register and set up your profile.
*   **User-Generated Content:** Details about the items you report as lost or found, including text descriptions, categories, and images you upload.
*   **Location Data:** Precise geolocation data (latitude and longitude) that you explicitly provide or select on the map when reporting an item. We do not track your location in the background.
*   **Communications:** Comments you post on item details and reports you submit through our in-app moderation system.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
*   **Facilitate the Service:** Display your lost/found reports and comments to the community.
*   **Smart Matching:** We use artificial intelligence (Google Gemini) to analyze images you upload of lost and found items in order to automatically notify you of potential matches.
*   **Safety & Moderation:** Review user reports to enforce our community guidelines, issue strikes, and ban users who violate our terms.
*   **Notifications:** Send you push notifications regarding activity on your posts or potential AI matches.

## 3. How We Share Your Information
*   **Community Visibility:** When you post an item or a comment, your username and the content are visible to other users. If you post a "Found" item, your profile contact details (if provided) may be displayed to help the owner reach you.
*   **Third-Party Service Providers:** We share necessary data with trusted third parties to run the app:
    *   **Supabase:** Our secure backend provider for authentication and database storage.
    *   **Google Maps Platform:** For rendering maps and geolocation services.
    *   **Google Gemini API:** For processing and comparing item images to find matches.

## 4. Data Security & Retention
Your data is securely stored using Supabase's Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure that only authorized users can access or modify specific information. You can delete your account at any time through the app settings, which will permanently remove your profile and associated data from our active database.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
